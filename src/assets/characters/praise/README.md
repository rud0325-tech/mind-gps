# Completion card smiles

These nine SVG variants are reserved for the final praise / power card. Initial character selection continues to use the original SVGs in the parent directory.

All nine mascots have upward-arched closed smiling eyes, drawn with rounded charcoal strokes. Eight variants have a warm open smile with a small pink tongue. The AI engineer smile is slightly narrower and lower to leave room for its glasses, and its smiling eyes remain inside the unchanged frames. Sports ace retains its existing toothy smile.

Only the mascot facial expressions are changed. Colors, silhouette, props, viewBox, and the animal guardian’s dog remain unchanged. The original assets are preserved.
